Science and technology

Before we build

Every plan set gets a sheet by sheet review before ground breaks. Then we meet each trade, before they are hired, to walk through scope, constraints, alternatives and risk. Those meetings settle who owns what, draw out better ways to build, and surface risks while they are still cheap to solve.

Systems that dry

Western Montana swings from deep cold to dry heat. Rainscreen cladding, an airtight envelope and controlled ventilation work as one system, so the structure stays dry and the air inside stays clean.

Numbers that tie

Our budgets come with their source material. Owners see the bids and quotes behind every line, and every contractor discount passes through to them. Complete transparency.

Integration is the SkyPac method

SkyPac works as an integrated construction manager, at the table while the project is still on paper.

We join the owner and design team during design, shape cost and schedule as the drawings develop, then build what we helped plan. We work this way on commercial, multifamily and custom residential projects.

  1. Design

    We estimate, schedule and value engineer alongside the design team, so cost shapes the drawings from the first sketch.

  2. Pricing

    Before construction starts, the project is priced on real bids, with the books open.

  3. Construction

    We manage the trades, the schedule and the budget through closeout.

Integration works best when the builder speaks two languages: the developer’s and the architect’s.
